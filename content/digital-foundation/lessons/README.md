# lessons/

Reserved for future longer-form lesson bodies (e.g. rich text/markdown
lesson pages) once a day's content needs more than the short
`lessonSummary` field in `days/day-NN.json`. In V1, Days 1–7 keep lesson
content inline in the day JSON — this folder exists to hold the structure
for when content authoring scales to the full 90 days without requiring a
schema change.
